# nova-horatia
A satire site inspired by Ancient Rome's great Horace. Better described as "The Reemergence of Horatian Satire"

A simple site made in HTML/CSS with themes based on Ancient Rome, especially inspired by satirical writers such as Horace. 
Not a complete project, but currently a small showcase of what it could be in regards to the three main sections:
* News - either news on myself (the writer), or parody news or retellings of old ancient Roman news.
* Satire - a mix of satire written by myself or actual translated satire from Ancient Rome.
* Lessons - a section where I, as a student of Latin, would write short sections about either the Latin language or culture.
