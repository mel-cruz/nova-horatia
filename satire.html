Why, Flacci, does society expect teens to act like adults
All while treating them like children,
And act confused when they choose to rebel?

It is truly a paradox of human standards.

Might we not be conditioned to be treated like adults
All while acting like the inner children we are?

Vale,
Nova Horatia.
